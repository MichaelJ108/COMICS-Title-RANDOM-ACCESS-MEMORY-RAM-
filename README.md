Title: RANDOM ACCESS MEMORY (RAM)

Created By: Leigh Henric Pantuab, Michael John Agocoy, Darren Hayes Aguirre Genre: Sci-Fi/Romance/Drama

Target Audience: Teenagers/Young Adults

SYNOPSIS

RANDOM ACCESS MEMORY follows Emma and James, two IT students whose once-happy relationship deteriorates under the weight of conflict and emotional exhaustion. Desperate to heal, they seek the experimental RAM procedure a system that targets and erases the memories of a specific person However, to delete a memory, they must first relive it. They must reconstruct their entire shared history, from the initial joyful spark to the small ente moments they took for granted. As they navigate their reconstructed past they face a painful truth is erasing their love a true fresh start, or are they losing the very thing that once defined them? Through this emosional final memory reconstruction, the story explores the choices we make to find peace and the nature of love in the face of scientific erasure
